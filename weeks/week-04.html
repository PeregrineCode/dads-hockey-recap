Week 4 Recap (2026-10-19 — 2026-10-25)

Tuesday, October 27, 2026

Matchup Recaps

by Murray "Muzz" Kowalchuk

The comeback of the week belongs to Pasta La Vista, who beat Fat Faulking Bussi 7-1-1 after trailing 1-7-1 on Tuesday. Bussi held the lead through Wednesday, then Pasta La Vista went up 5-4-0 on Thursday and never gave it back. The final tally was 17 goals, 38 points and a 2.26 GAA to Bussi's 8 goals, 28 points and a 3.37 GAA. Bussi's 59 hits won that category and nothing else came with them. Tyler's goalies and finishers shut the door in the second half of the week, and Ivan watched an early lead slip away.

Hacklin Celeweenie edged Nette's Sweaty Yetis 4-3-2 and won it on the final day. Nette's led from Tuesday on, 6-3-0 for the first stretch and 5-3-1 on Friday and Saturday, and Hacklin trailed by three categories at one point. Then Sunday flipped it. Hacklin's week ended at 63 hits, a 2.13 GAA and a .917 save percentage, while Nette's finished with the better offence at 33 points to 24. Mac's hits and goaltending outlasted Andrew's scoring, and the SOG category came down to 79 shots to 78. A single shot decided that one.

Jacks in the Box survived 50 in '07 4-3-2 in a seesaw with two lead changes. Dennis's club led 3-2-4 on Monday, fell behind 3-5-1 Tuesday and Wednesday, edged back in front 5-3-1 on Thursday and sat tied at 4-4-1 on Saturday. Sunday finished it. The week's final tally has Jacks in the Box winning goals, points, shots and blocks (99 shots to 96), while Derek's side took hits, GAA and save percentage, 38 hits to 28. A .874 save percentage and a 3.58 GAA are the kind of goaltending numbers that usually lose a week, and goals and shots on net rescued it.

Fortune Favors The Boldy put Jetting out of Winnipeg away 7-2-0, though Cole's club led early, up 5-0-4 on Monday and still ahead 5-4-0 on Friday. Jim's team came back with 108 shots and 62 hits, took goals, assists, points, shots, hits, GAA and save percentage, and left Winnipeg with blocks and wins only. On The Bench Hockey 2.0 ran down Ducks on the Pond 🦆 by the same 7-2-0 count after the Ducks opened 4-0-5. Jordan's goaltending did the damage, a 1.50 GAA and a .952 save percentage. The Ducks took assists and points and nothing else.

Gavin Mackinaw beat Hertl the turtle 6-3-0, and the narrowest category was save percentage, .884 to .880. Jean-Christophe's club won shots, hits and blocks (93, 61 and 37) but went winless in goal with a 4.15 GAA and no wins. Jesse's side took the scoring categories, with 39 points to 24, and the one win in net.

Mackin' a Drai Hard held off Oscar Meier Wiener 5-4-0 on goals, assists, points, GAA and save percentage. Dave's club won shots, hits, blocks and wins, 101 shots to 93 and 50 hits to 29, but a .866 save percentage was too deep a hole. Matthew's club scored 18 goals to 9 and 44 points to 31, and that was the whole difference.

Players of the Week

by Murray "Muzz" Kowalchuk

McDavid's week was the kind of thing that makes a lifelong rink rat put down his coffee. Ten points on two goals and eight assists, ten shots, and the Gavin Mackinaw centre did it while also chipping in four hits and two blocks, which is more defensive honesty than most of the league's top-line forwards will give you in a month. Eight helpers tells you everything about how EDM runs through him: he drags three sticks toward himself and then finds the open man. Gavin Mackinaw gets the Skater of the Week trophy, and Jesse earned every ounce of it. Nobody had a bigger single-player week.

The irony is that the guy on the other end of those passes had a week nearly as loud. Draisaitl, rostered by Mackin' a Drai Hard, banged home six goals and fired 15 shots, a trigger-happy volume that would make a sniper from the old days blush. Panarin put up four goals, three assists and 13 shots for 50 in '07, and Drake Batherson of OTT quietly ran a full-service week for Nette's Sweaty Yetis with 3 goals, 3 assists, 7 shots, 6 hits and 4 blocks. That last line is the one that sticks, because six hits and four blocks next to the scoring is how you win categories nobody else is paying attention to.

In goal, Joel Hofer of STL takes the crown for 50 in '07: two wins, a 1.50 GAA and a .951 save percentage on 58 saves from 61 shots. Three goals against all week, and it makes it a double-dip week for Derek's club with Panarin in the mix as well. Pyotr Kochetkov of CAR matched the 1.50 GAA with two wins for Fortune Favors The Boldy, stopping 38 of 41 for a .927. Jacob Fowler of MTL was sharp for Pasta La Vista, a .952 on 20 saves from 21 shots with one win, while Jake Oettinger of DAL piled up three wins for Jetting out of Winnipeg despite a .881 and a 2.25 GAA. Wins are wins, and in a nine-category league, three of them is a real head start.

Power Rankings

by Murray "Muzz" Kowalchuk

Contenders

Four clubs with real separation from the pack, and every one of them won this week. This is who I'd want to avoid in a seven-game series.

#1 Pasta La Vista — 23-10-3 (.681) — W, 7-1-1

Seven categories in the bag and only one dropped. That's a team that wins the grind categories and the glamour ones alike, and the best record in the league says the same thing.

#2 On The Bench Hockey 2.0 — 23-11-2 (.667) — W, 7-2-0

Seven to two over the week, and the club sits one loss off the top. Jordan has this thing humming, and I'd hate to be the team drawing it in the quarterfinal.

#3 Fortune Favors The Boldy — 22-11-3 (.653) — W, 7-2-0

Another 7-2-0 win, and Jim's club keeps stacking them. Boring and repeatable, and that's how you build a playoff seed.

#4 Mackin’ a Drai Hard — 23-13-0 (.639) — W, 5-4-0

A 5-4-0 squeaker is still two points in the bank. Matthew has the same winning percentage as the club below him and one more win, so he holds the spot, but he'd better start winning these by more than a whisker.

Solid

Good teams with playoff aspirations and a few holes. Any of them can climb with a good month, and any of them can slide with a bad one.

#5 Oscar Meier Wiener — 22-12-2 (.639) — L, 4-5-0

A one-category loss is the kind that stings but doesn't wound. Dave still owns a strong record, and he needs to stop leaving margin on the table in the close weeks.

#6 Hacklin Celeweenie — 20-13-3 (.597) — W, 4-3-2

Four wins, three losses and two ties, and still a win. Mac took a messy week and came out the right side of it, which is what the good teams do.

#7 Jacks in the Box — 18-14-4 (.556) — W, 4-3-2

Same 4-3-2 result as the club above, and the same happy ending. Dennis has four ties on the season, so he's been living in the gaps all year. He's still on the right side of .500.

Mediocre

The middle of the pack, where a hot week and a cold week can swap places on the board. Nobody here has earned a comfortable seat.

#8 Gavin Mackinaw — 17-18-1 (.486) — W, 6-3-0

A 6-3-0 win is the best result among this group, and Jesse needed it to stay near the line. The record is still a game under .500, so one good week doesn't fix the slide.

#9 50 in ‘07 — 15-18-3 (.458) — L, 3-4-2

A 3-4-2 week: hits, GAA and save percentage won, two categories tied, and none of the scoring categories came with them. It's a week that makes you wonder where the offence went, and Derek has some work to do.

#10 Jetting out of Winnipeg — 17-19-0 (.472) — L, 2-7-0

A 2-7-0 beating is how a team drops below the line in a hurry. Cole sits at 17-19-0 and needs a full reset on the blue line and in the crease.

#11 Nette's Sweaty Yetis — 12-18-6 (.417) — L, 3-4-2

Six ties on the season tells the story of a team that can't quite finish the job. Andrew had a chance to take this week and gave it back.

Rebuilding

The bottom three are looking at the playoff race through the glass. Time to think about the trade market and next year's draft.

#12 Hertl the turtle — 10-26-0 (.278) — L, 3-6-0

Ten wins against twenty-six losses is a long climb, even with most of the season left. Jean-Christophe keeps losing the way a team does when the lineup isn't clicking.

#13 Ducks on the Pond 🦆 — 7-27-2 (.222) — L, 2-7-0

A 2-7-0 loss on top of a deep record hole. The flock is grounded for now, and week 4 is too early to call it anything more.

#14 Fat Faulking Bussi — 7-26-3 (.236) — L, 1-7-1

One category won all week, and the record backs up the ugly picture. Ivan needs a rebuild, because the roster as constructed is not going to dig itself out.

Best Pickup of the Week

by Murray "Muzz" Kowalchuk

The Ducks on the Pond 🦆 get the nod for the best pickup of the week. Charle-Edouard D'Astous (D, TB) came off the wire and gave Ducks on the Pond 🦆 a goal, two assists, three points, five shots, six hits and four blocks. Transaction Desk

by Murray "Muzz" Kowalchuk

Two trades went down on October 20, and I'll take the one that matters most. Mackin' a Drai Hard got Nico Hischier (C, NJ) for Vasily Podkolzin (LW,RW, EDM) going to Fortune Favors The Boldy. I like Matthew's side. Hischier is a proven top-line centre, and centre depth is hard to find. Podkolzin has the shots and hits to be useful, but a skater with that profile is easier to replace than a first-line pivot. Jim gets a decent winger, and Matthew gets the player who moves the needle.

The other deal sent Scott Wedgewood (G, COL) to Hertl the turtle and Elias Pettersson (C, VAN) to Ducks on the Pond 🦆. This one is closer. Goalies who hold a starting job are the scarcest asset in the format, and Jean-Christophe now has one. Tom gets a centre who should produce, but a starter in net is worth more to me than a skater. I'll lean to the turtle on this deal, though Wedgewood has to hold the job to make me right.

On the FAAB side, only one bid went through this week. Jacks in the Box spent $8 on Roman Kantserov (RW, CHI), dropping Mason Marchment, and got a goal, a point, six shots and a hit out of it. That is a modest return on a small bid, and Dennis sits at $55 remaining. The budgets to watch are elsewhere. Fortune Favors The Boldy is down to $0, Mackin' a Drai Hard has $3 left, and Ducks on the Pond 🦆 has $7. That is three teams with almost no ammunition and a long season left, and a goalie emergency in February will leave them stuck with whatever the wire gives them for free. Ten other clubs sit at $85 or better: Fat Faulking Bussi has $85, and Pasta La Vista, On The Bench Hockey 2.0, Oscar Meier Wiener, Hacklin Celeweenie, Gavin Mackinaw, Jetting out of Winnipeg, 50 in '07, Nette's Sweaty Yetis and Hertl the turtle have not spent a dollar.

The Trade Board

by Gary "The Wire" Halloran

Biggest name on the board this week: I'm told Nikita Kucherov has requested a trade. Even better, he has submitted exactly one destination, and it's Jacks in the Box. The word is he wants to land somewhere with a winning culture. Dennis's club sits seventh at 18-14-4, so there's a case that the culture is still being built. I can't confirm what the front office has offered or whether it has picked up the phone. A one-team list is a rare thing, and every rival general manager in this league should be watching that inbox.

Here's how the dots connect. Jacks in the Box is a playoff-position team and not a juggernaut. Pasta La Vista, On The Bench Hockey 2.0 and Fortune Favors The Boldy all sit above it, and a player of Kucherov's calibre is exactly what you add if you plan to climb. The same front office put in an $8 bid on Roman Kantserov, which tells me it is shopping at every price point. Keep an eye on what leaves the building in a Kucherov deal, because that's where the real story lives.

Second item, and the phones are busy: Ivan is shopping Vasi. I'm hearing multiple offers have gone out this week alone, which is not how a manager behaves when he's casually testing the market. Fat Faulking Bussi is 7-26-3, thirteenth in the standings, and a club in that spot has little reason to hold a valuable asset. I'm not naming the full player, and I can't confirm yet who is on the other end of those offers. If you're a contender with a hole in net, you already know who you are.

The recent paper trail shows the market is moving. Fortune Favors The Boldy landed Vasily Podkolzin while Mackin' a Drai Hard got Nico Hischier. Hertl the turtle brought in Scott Wedgewood, and Ducks on the Pond 🦆 picked up Elias Pettersson. The bottom of the table is clearly open for business, and the trade deadline is March 3. That gives these deals just over four months to develop.

Hearing more by midweek. Keep your phone charged.

That's the Trade Board. The Wire is always listening.

The Fancy Stats Desk

by Dr. Ingrid Lindqvist

Hacklin Celeweenie put up 63 hits, the league high, and a .917 save percentage with a 2.13 GAA, and the result was a 4-3-2 squeeze past Nette's Sweaty Yetis. The cost was in the scoring columns. Hacklin Celeweenie managed 14 assists, the league low against an average of 20.07, plus 10 goals and 24 points. The Yetis had 21 assists and 33 points. Mac's club took shots, hits and both goalie ratios, tied blocks at 39 and wins at 2, and still dropped goals, assists and points. A team can dominate the physical and goaltending categories and leave a draw on the table because it has no finish.

The mirror image is Oscar Meier Wiener. Dave's club posted 3 wins, and Jetting out of Winnipeg matched that figure. Both did it on poor ratios. Oscar's .866 save percentage was the league low against an average of .894, with a 3.48 GAA. Winnipeg's .869 was close behind. Neither goalie group was good, but wins are a count, and counts reward volume and run support. Oscar still lost 4-5-0 to Mackin' a Drai Hard. Winnipeg lost 2-7-0 to Fortune Favors The Boldy, with a league-low 6 goals and 22 points. A win total built on a .866 save percentage is the first number I would expect to regress.

At the other end sits On The Bench Hockey 2.0. A 1.50 GAA and a .952 save percentage are the best marks in the league this week, and the average GAA is 2.89. The 7-2-0 result against Ducks on the Pond 🦆 is the expected outcome for a goalie pair that clean. The Ducks led the league with 27 assists and still went without a goalie win. Their 2.94 GAA sits almost exactly on the league average. Goaltending at that level sets a floor under the week, and the skaters add 91 shots and 45 hits, right around the league averages of 91.07 and 43.36. A .952 save percentage across a week is a hot stretch, though, and it will cool.

One more line worth a glance is Jacks in the Box, who posted 18 goals, tied with Mackin' a Drai Hard for the top figure. That is well above the 12.07 average. A .874 save percentage and a 3.58 GAA left Dennis's club at 4-3-2, with the goals offset by the goalie categories. Eighteen goals on 99 shots is a shooting percentage that won't hold, and the sample is a single week.
