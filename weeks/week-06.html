Week 6 Recap (2026-11-02 — 2026-11-08)

Monday, November 9, 2026

Matchup Recaps

by Murray "Muzz" Kowalchuk

The record book got a new top line this week, and the team that wrote it still took the loss. Ducks on the Pond 🦆 piled up 66 points on 18 goals and 48 assists, the most any team has put up in a single week in the ten-year history of this league. Nobody else topped 47, and the Ducks' own 49 last week had been the season high. Then they lost to Bussi was too Drai by a 5-3-1 count. The Ducks swept goals, assists and points, but their goalies posted a .870 save percentage and a 3.22 GAA against Bussi's .927 and 1.95. Bussi was too Drai took shots on goal (115 to 106), hits (43 to 32), blocks (55 to 41), GAA and save percentage, and wins tied 2-2. I've seen teams get run out of the building with a record in their pocket before, but not one that buries the scoresheet this deep. Goaltending still decides this league, and 66 points can't cover a .870.

Two matchups finished with the better offence losing the goalie battle. Boeser's Castle beat Hacklin Celeweenie 6-3-0 on the strength of a skater sweep: goals, assists, points, shots (102 to 90), hits (74 to 49) and blocks (41 to 39). The goalie line makes no sense. Boeser's Castle finished with no wins, an 8.87 GAA and a .650 save percentage, while Hacklin Celeweenie ran a 1.50 GAA and a .939. Boeser's Castle held the lead for most of the week. Hacklin Celeweenie grabbed it on Saturday, and a Sunday swing handed it back for good. Winning 6-3-0 with that crease is a lucky escape.

On The Bench Hockey 2.0 came from behind to flatten Nette's Sweaty Yetis 7-0-2. Nette's Sweaty Yetis led through the first two days. On The Bench Hockey 2.0 took over on the Wednesday and never gave the lead back, pulling away from there. The Yetis finished without a single category win, with goals and wins tied. On The Bench Hockey 2.0 blocked 48 shots to 31, won assists 23 to 12, and ran a 2.39 GAA to a 4.04.

LL Cooley J was in front from the first day and never looked back, beating Oscar Meier Wiener 7-2-0. The skaters took goals (17 to 15), assists (30 to 16), points, shots (119 to 109) and hits (61 to 41). Goaltending was the closer: LL Cooley J posted a 1.50 GAA and a .945 save percentage against 1.99 and .930. Oscar Meier Wiener took wins (5 to 2) and blocks (41 to 37), and nothing else.

50 in '07 beat Jetting out of Winnipeg 6-2-1 behind a .931 save percentage and a 2.05 GAA against a .831 and a 4.38. 50 in '07 also outshot Winnipeg 115 to 85. Jetting out of Winnipeg salvaged blocks by a single shot (31 to 30) and wins, 3 to 0, and goals tied at 11 apiece.

Gavin Mackinaw edged Loosy Kuchy 5-4-0 in a game decided by one category. Gavin Mackinaw took assists, shots, wins, GAA and save percentage. Loosy Kuchy won goals, points, hits (52 to 51) and blocks despite a 4.08 GAA and no wins from its goalies. A .906 against a .857 in the crease was the difference.

Fortune Favors The Boldy and Pasta La Vista finished 4-4-1, with shots on goal tied at 101 apiece. Fortune Favors The Boldy took goals, hits (58 to 35), wins and GAA. Pasta La Vista answered with assists, points, blocks and a .909 to .901 edge in save percentage, the closest category of the matchup. Neither side can say it deserved better.

Players of the Week

by Murray "Muzz" Kowalchuk

Cutter Gauthier of Ducks on the Pond 🦆 takes the skater crown, and the margin is wide. Eight goals and two assists made 10 points, on 19 shots. He is a young Anaheim forward who looks like he has stopped asking permission. He was eligible at C, LW and RW, and every slot he touched got filled. Gauthier supplied 10 of his club's record 66 points, and it still wasn't enough to win the matchup. That is a hockey injustice, and I'm not giving the award back.

The runners-up were strong. LL Cooley J got Jack Hughes at 3 goals, 6 assists, 19 shots and 3 blocks for 9 points. Anybody who gets that many shots and a few blocks out of a Devils centre is playing the right way. Brady Tkachuk of Fortune Favors The Boldy posted 5 points, 20 shots and 19 hits. That is a Tkachuk week, with the Florida forward hitting everything in sight and still scoring. Tomas Hertl of Boeser's Castle had 4 goals, 2 assists, 13 shots, 15 hits and 2 blocks. I love a Vegas forward who throws his weight around.

In goal, Connor Hellebuyck wins it for Fortune Favors The Boldy. He had 3 wins, 69 saves on 76 shots, a 2.26 GAA and a .908 save percentage. The ratios aren't the prettiest on this list, but nobody else came close to his workload or his win total, and he is the one who got his manager three wins. Jeremy Swayman (BOS) and Dylan Garand (NYR) both belong to Oscar Meier Wiener. Swayman went 2 wins with a 1.44 GAA and a .953 save percentage, and Garand went 2 wins with a 1.50 GAA and a .948. That is a pair of goalies who did their job. Cam Talbot (CBJ) completes the field for Gavin Mackinaw, with 2 wins, a 1.44 GAA and a .951 save percentage. Talbot has been around forever, and he still knows how to stop a puck.

Best Pickup of the Week

by Murray "Muzz" Kowalchuk

Jamie Drysdale takes the top spot, and Loosy Kuchy gets the credit for it. The PHI defenceman played two games, both in the active lineup, and finished with 2 assists, 5 shots, a hit and 6 blocks. Ask any old defenceman what a good night looks like and he'll tell you it ends with a pile of blocked shots and a few points to show for it. Drysdale's week was exactly that, and in a league that scores blocks, it's a joy to see that kind of line pay off.

Brett Pesce is the runner-up for LL Cooley J. The New Jersey defenceman played three games in the active lineup and finished with 3 assists, 4 shots, a hit and 3 blocks. Pesce is a stay-at-home defenceman who does the dirty work for a living, and three points from a waiver-wire blueliner is a good use of a roster spot.

Loosy Kuchy doubled up, too. Dmitry Orlov of SJ played four games and posted 3 assists, 2 shots, a hit and 2 blocks. Orlov is a veteran who has seen everything the league can throw at him, and he gave the manager steady production across a full slate. Two useful defencemen off the wire in one week, with a combined 5 points and 8 blocks, is how you build a roster from the bottom up.

Goalie Stream of the Week

by Murray "Muzz" Kowalchuk

Tristan Jarry of EDM is the only goalie on the board this week, and Bussi was too Drai gets the nod for streaming him. In one start he stopped 31 of 34 shots for a .912 save percentage and a 2.78 GAA. He didn't pick up the win, so the stream didn't deliver the full package, but it was a solid line in a spot where a bad start could have wrecked a week.

A .912 on a heavy night of 34 shots against is the sort of reliable, no-drama outing a stream is supposed to give you. It won't make the highlight reel, but it kept the ratios in good shape, and Bussi was too Drai finished the week with a .927 save percentage and a 1.95 GAA.

Streaming a goalie is a gamble, and the safest bet is the one that doesn't hurt you. Jarry cost nothing in terms of damage and gave this team a fair shot at the goalie categories. A manager who held the line in net deserves the credit.

Transaction Desk

by Murray "Muzz" Kowalchuk

The FAAB desk was quiet this week with only $9 spent across the league. LL Cooley J made the biggest move, bidding $8 on Karel Vejmelka of UTA and dropping Zach Whitecloud. That's 8% of the starting budget on a goalie, which is a reasonable price when a manager needs a net. Bussi was too Drai went the other way with a $1 bid on Mason McTavish of STL, dropping Charlie Coyle. A dollar for a young centre is a cheap flier, and the kind of bid that costs nothing if it doesn't pan out.

The budget picture is more interesting than the bidding. Fortune Favors The Boldy is at $0 remaining, with nothing left to spend on a goalie emergency or a hot waiver add. Loosy Kuchy has $3 left and Ducks on the Pond 🦆 has $7. Those three have burned through almost everything this early in the season, and a long schedule is ahead of them. A manager with an empty wallet can still pick up free agents who don't carry a bid, but the real fights will be lost.

At the other end, eight teams have not spent a dollar and sit at the full $100. Gavin Mackinaw is at $44, LL Cooley J at $47 and Bussi was too Drai at $84. Plenty of managers are sitting on a war chest, and the ones with cash have the leverage when the injuries start to pile up.

The Fancy Stats Desk

by Dr. Ingrid Lindqvist

Ducks on the Pond 🦆 show the ceiling on scoring in a nine-category format. Their 18 goals and 48 assists, against league averages of 12.14 and 24.07, bought exactly three categories. Bussi was too Drai took shots, hits and blocks (55, the week's top figure), the Ducks' .870 save percentage against a league average of .884 handed over both goalie ratios, wins tied, and the final was 5-3-1.

Boeser's Castle supplies the reverse test of how much skater volume can carry. Boeser's Castle went 6-3-0 over Hacklin Celeweenie with zero wins, an 8.87 GAA and a .650 save percentage, all the worst in the league. The skaters won all six of their categories: 74 hits, the league high, plus 13 goals and 102 shots. The goalie line barely registered because it only had three categories to lose, and it lost all three. Hacklin's 1.50 GAA tied for the league's best mark and it still lost the matchup. A goalie can be flawless and the team can still go 3-6-0.

Wins are the noisiest category on the board. The league averaged 2 per team. Oscar Meier Wiener and Fortune Favors The Boldy each had 5, the week's high. Fortune turned it into a 4-4-1 tie with Pasta La Vista. Oscar lost 7-2-0 to LL Cooley J, who won with 2 wins on a league-best .945 save percentage and 119 shots. Meanwhile 50 in '07 posted a 2.05 GAA and a .931 save percentage and collected zero wins, then dropped the category to Jetting out of Winnipeg, who won 3 behind an .831 save percentage. Wins track goal support and workload far more than goaltending quality. Any manager chasing wins without watching the schedule is chasing noise.

One more line worth a second look is On The Bench Hockey 2.0's 7-0-2. Their 33 points sat below the league average of 36.21. They won because Nette's Sweaty Yetis posted the league low in assists (12) and points (22), and tied the Ducks for the fewest hits (32). Sometimes the 7-0-2 is built on the opponent's collapse. Sample size, as ever.
