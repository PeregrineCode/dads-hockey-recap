Matchup Recaps

by Murray "Muzz" Kowalchuk

Pasta La Vista spent the week chasing LL Cooley J and caught it on the last day. Cooley led every day through Friday, and the arc shows 6-1-2 on Monday and 7-2-0 on Tuesday from Cooley's side. Saturday finally tied it at 4-4-1. Sunday tipped it to a 5-4-0 final for Pasta La Vista. The winners took goals, points, shots on goal, hits and wins. LL Cooley J took assists, blocks and both goalie rate categories, a 1.67 GAA and a .937 save percentage. That is a hard pair of numbers to lose with. Five wins to four in the W column decided it, and Pasta La Vista edged the shot count 104 to 97. Tyler's club gets the Game 7 overtime treatment, and Dennis's club gets the long ride home.

Ducks on the Pond 🦆 came from behind against Loosy Kuchy. Loosy Kuchy led Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday, at one point 5-0-4 up in the arc. Thursday flipped it to 7-2-0 for Ducks on the Pond 🦆, and Tom's crew held it from there to a 6-3-0 final. They did it with 109 shots on goal, 12 goals and 31 points. The goaltending was a disaster, a 4.78 GAA and a .851 save percentage, and they still won the wins category 1 to 0. Matthew's Loosy Kuchy took hits, GAA and save percentage and had nothing to show for it.

Hacklin Celeweenie beat Bussi was too Drai 5-3-1, and Mac's goalies did the work. Bussi was too Drai led Monday and Tuesday. Wednesday handed the lead to Hacklin Celeweenie, which never gave it back. The winners posted a 1.79 GAA and a .933 save percentage with five wins, while taking assists and shots. Ivan's side won goals, hits and blocks, 44 of the last to 20, and tied points at 27 apiece. It was a good week of stay-at-home hockey from Bussi was too Drai, and it lost anyway.

Gavin Mackinaw edged Fortune Favors The Boldy 5-4-0 despite one win to three. Jesse's crew took goals, points, blocks, GAA and save percentage, with 36 blocks to 13. Jim's Fortune Favors The Boldy won assists, shots (86 to 84), hits and wins. Those SOG and hit numbers say the offence was fine and the net let him down, with a .882 save percentage.

Nette's Sweaty Yetis beat Jetting out of Winnipeg 5-4-0 on elite goaltending. Andrew's goalies put up a 1.47 GAA and a .955 save percentage, and the Yetis added assists, points and wins. Cole's Jetting out of Winnipeg took goals (6 to 5), shots, hits and blocks. It had a 3.27 GAA and a .885 save percentage, which is how a team wins four skater categories and still loses.

50 in ‘07 went wire to wire against Oscar Meier Wiener for a 7-1-1 win. Derek's club led from Monday morning and hit 9-0-0 on Tuesday. The week ended at 28 assists, 39 points and 111 shots, with a 1.95 GAA and a .932 save percentage. Dave's Oscar Meier Wiener won hits, 65 to 46, and tied wins at 2.

On The Bench Hockey 2.0 swept Dying on this Hill 9-0-0, the only sweep of the week. Jordan's team led all week, a 9-0-0 on Tuesday and again on Sunday. It had 17 goals, 22 assists and 49 blocks, and won the wins category 1 to 0. Dying on this Hill put up a 4.28 GAA and a .828 save percentage, which tells the story. Jean-Christophe's week had nothing to build on.

Players of the Week

by Murray "Muzz" Kowalchuk

Kyle Connor of WPG takes the skater crown for Pasta La Vista. Four goals, three assists, seven points and 15 shots on net across the week, with two hits and two blocks thrown in, which is more rink-level honesty than most scorers bother with. Connor has been a pure shooter for years, a winger who gets to the same soft spot in the slot and leans on the release. The tiebreaker was close. Leon Draisaitl of EDM posted the same 4-3-7 and the same 15 shots for Bussi was too Drai, and he had a block more. Connor gets the nod for the hit column. Nobody in this league should lose sleep over that ruling.

The runners-up filled out a strong podium. Brandon Hagel of TB gave Dying on this Hill four goals, two assists and six points on 15 shots, plus three hits and a block. That is a full lunch-pail line from a guy who plays like he's still fighting for a roster spot. Macklin Celebrini of SJ had two goals, four assists and 17 shots for Hacklin Celeweenie, the most pucks thrown at the net of anyone named here. He's a young centre who keeps showing up in these write-ups, and I'm not complaining.

In goal, Devon Levi of EDM wins it for LL Cooley J. He stopped 49 of 50 shots, allowed one goal, and finished with a 0.50 GAA, a .980 save percentage and two wins. That is the kind of week that wins two goalie categories before the weekend even starts. Hacklin Celeweenie gets a second nod, this time for Jesper Wallstedt of MIN: three wins, 84 saves on 89 shots, a 1.66 GAA and a .944 save percentage. That is a big workload, and he held up under it. Jacob Markstrom of FLA gave Nette's Sweaty Yetis two wins, a 1.00 GAA and a .969 save percentage on 64 shots against. Tristan Jarry of EDM rounds it out for Bussi was too Drai with two wins and a .942 save percentage, so that team got a skater and a goalie onto the board this week.

Power Rankings

by Murray "Muzz" Kowalchuk

Contenders

The class of the league, and the gap between the top and everyone else is something a lifer can see from the press box. Four clubs, four different ways of getting it done.

#1 On The Bench Hockey 2.0 — 44-14-5 (.738) — W, 9-0-0

Nine-nothing, a lead held from Monday to Sunday, and a 44-14-5 record that nobody else in this league can touch. Jordan has built a machine, and this is the team everyone else is chasing.

A 5-4-0 squeaker is the kind of win that wins championships, because it means they found a way on a night when the legs weren't there. Tyler's club has the second-best record in the league and keeps stacking wins without making a fuss about it.

#3 50 in ‘07 — 34-23-6 (.587) — W, 7-1-1

A 7-1-1 beating is a statement from Derek's group, and it moves them up the board. Seven categories is a full week of work and I respect every hit and block that went into it.

Jesse's team scraped out a 5-4-0 win, and in this league a win is a win. At 33-29-1 they're in the mix, but they have to start winning these by a wider margin.

#9 Jetting out of Winnipeg — 26-36-1 (.421) — L, 4-5-0

Cole's team lost 4-5-0, a result that was in the balance right to the end. They're 26-36-1 now, and the playoff chase is getting steeper by the week.

#11 Ducks on the Pond 🦆 — 21-39-3 (.357) — W, 6-3-0

A 6-3-0 win is the best result Tom's group has had in a while and they climb out of the basement tier because of it. At 21-39-3 there's still a mountain to climb, but this is a start.

Transaction Desk

Tyler got two forwards, both with Yahoo eligibility at C/RW or LW/RW, and both are the kind of players who fill a lineup without needing a miracle. It is an even swap that fills a need on both sides. Winner of the week: Pasta La Vista, who made two trades and came out of both with real pieces.

The Fancy Stats Desk

by Dr. Ingrid Lindqvist

The week's cleanest oddity is in the LL Cooley J and Pasta La Vista matchup. LL Cooley J posted a 1.67 GAA and a .937 save percentage, against league averages of 2.71 and .900. Both figures beat Pasta La Vista's 2.64 and .894, and the matchup still finished 5-4-0 the other way. The cause was the wins category, 5 to 4, plus a skater edge for Pasta La Vista of 14 goals to 6 and 104 shots to 97. A goalie line that good cannot repeat, and a team that scores 6 goals on 97 shots will not stay at 6.

Nette's Sweaty Yetis is the same story, only more extreme. Their 1.47 GAA and .955 save percentage were the best in the league, and their 5 goals were the fewest. They won 5-4-0 on assists, points, wins and the two rate stats. The skaters lost goals, shots, hits and blocks to Jetting out of Winnipeg. Their 71 shots were the second-fewest in the league. A .955 goalie line is sample size, and the rest of the roster shows no sign of it.

The reverse case belongs to Ducks on the Pond 🦆. They went 6-3-0 despite a 4.78 GAA and an .851 save percentage, the worst GAA in the league and the second-worst save percentage. The skaters did the work, with 109 shots against a league average of 88.57, and the team took goals, assists, points, shots, blocks and the single win. Their 31 hits were the lowest in the league and lost that category anyway. A team can survive one dreadful goalie week if the shot volume is that high, though a week that thin in hits and a goalie line like that rarely holds up twice.

On The Bench Hockey 2.0 went 9-0-0 with a goalie line of 1.99 and .907, which is respectable and unremarkable. The sweep came from 17 goals and 49 blocks, both league highs. Dying on this Hill hit the wrong end of the same distribution with a 4.28 GAA, a .828 save percentage and zero wins. The .828 was the league's worst. Nine categories decided that matchup, and none of them was close to a coin flip.
