Week 5 Recap (2026-10-26 — 2026-11-01)

Monday, November 2, 2026

Matchup Recaps

by Murray "Muzz" Kowalchuk

Pasta La Vista opened the week 7-0-2 down to Loosy Kuchy and finished it 8-0-1 up, winning every category but assists, which ended tied. They took the lead on Tuesday and never gave it back. The goaltending decided it: two wins, a 1.01 GAA and a .971 save percentage, against 3.37 and .865 for Loosy Kuchy. Add 16 goals, 60 hits and 34 blocks and Tyler had a blowout that began as a disaster. Loosy Kuchy went from owning the scoreboard on Monday to taking home nothing at all.

The closest result of the week was Ducks on the Pond 🦆 over Angels and Dmen, 5-4-0, and it needed a comeback. Angels and Dmen led from Monday through Friday, up 6-0-3 after the first day. Ducks on the Pond 🦆 took the lead Saturday and held it through Sunday, winning goals, assists, points, shots and blocks. The goaltending was ugly: no wins, an 8.68 GAA and a .737 save percentage. The skaters did enough that none of it mattered. Angels and Dmen took hits by a single one, 47 to 46, the closest category of the night. Tom survived on a pile of skater production, and I can't recommend it as a method.

LL Cooley J beat Jetting out of Winnipeg 6-3-0, and the lead changed hands twice on the way. The week started tied, LL Cooley J went up Tuesday, Jetting out of Winnipeg flipped it Wednesday, and LL Cooley J took it back Thursday and kept it. Dennis's skaters won all six skater categories, with 23 goals and 107 shots on the week. Jetting out of Winnipeg won the three goalie categories behind a 2.79 GAA and a .888 save percentage, a margin of .013 over a .875 mark that kept the goalie side from being a rout. Cole's team was the better goaltending team, and Dennis's team was the better skating team by a wide margin.

50 in '07 took Nette's Sweaty Yetis 6-2-1 in a seesaw. 50 in '07 led Monday, Nette's Sweaty Yetis flipped it Tuesday, and Derek's side went back up on Wednesday and stayed there. The goalie line was 1.50 and .950 against 3.47 and .856. Nette's Sweaty Yetis won assists by one, 25 to 24, plus the wins category with three, and the two teams tied on points at 35. 50 in '07 won the shot battle 101 to 73 and the blocks 31 to 15, and the gap in the crease did the rest.

Fortune Favors The Boldy beat Oscar Meier Wiener 6-2-1 after trailing on the opening day. Oscar Meier Wiener led 6-1-2 after Monday, then Fortune Favors The Boldy went in front on Tuesday and stayed there. Jim's goalies carried the week: three wins, a 1.33 GAA and a .933 save percentage. They also had 18 goals to 5 and 66 hits to 53. Oscar Meier Wiener won shots by one, 97 to 96, and blocks 40 to 21, with assists tied at 15. Dave's team won the crease-front battle and lost anyway.

Gavin Mackinaw beat Your team's Aho 6-3-0 on goaltending. Jesse's side took wins, GAA and save percentage with a 1.96 GAA and a .931 save percentage, and added shots, hits and blocks. The blocks went 36 to 35, which tells you how close the whole thing was. Your team's Aho won goals, assists and points with 14 goals and 39 points, but a 3.84 GAA and a .852 save percentage left Ivan with three scoring categories and nothing behind the net.

On The Bench Hockey 2.0 beat Hacklin Celeweenie 5-3-1 in a game of two halves. Jordan's skaters won assists, points, shots, hits and blocks with 109 shots, 61 hits and 47 blocks, and goals ended tied at 11. Hacklin Celeweenie took all three goalie categories with two wins, a 1.99 GAA and a .925 save percentage, but Mac's skaters couldn't keep up. On The Bench Hockey 2.0 had a 4.36 GAA and no wins and still took the matchup, because the skaters scored five of the six categories they could.

Players of the Week

by Murray "Muzz" Kowalchuk

Tage Thompson of Hacklin Celeweenie takes the skater crown. Five goals and three assists for eight points, on 18 shots, with three blocks thrown in for a forward who doesn't usually bother. Eighteen shots on goal is a pile of rubber, the kind of volume that has an opposing goalie asking for a different sweater number. Thompson has always had the big shot and the big frame in Buffalo, and this week BUF's centre used both on every shift. Mac's team also has Wallstedt among the goalie runners-up, so that is a lot of good news landing in one lineup.

Cutter Gauthier of Ducks on the Pond 🦆 (ANA) is the runner-up, matching Thompson's five goals with an assist on 19 shots, plus three hits and a block. That is a sniper's week, and it is a big reason Tom finally got a win. Evan Bouchard of On The Bench Hockey 2.0 (EDM) had a goal and four assists with 12 shots, three hits and seven blocks, which is a lot of work in both ends from a defenceman. Joel Farabee of Your team's Aho (CGY) piled up three goals and three assists with nine shots, four hits and three blocks. That is a winger who plays the full 200 feet, and I respect it.

In goal, Jacob Fowler of Pasta La Vista (MTL) stopped all 46 shots he faced in the lineup for a win and a perfect 1.000 save percentage. Forty-six saves without one getting by is a week where the goalie earns his groceries, and that line speaks for itself. Pyotr Kochetkov of Fortune Favors The Boldy (CAR) had two wins, a 1.50 GAA and a .929 save percentage on 42 shots. Jesper Wallstedt of Hacklin Celeweenie (MIN) was the workhorse, with two wins, a 1.99 GAA and a .925 save percentage on 80 shots against. Joel Hofer of 50 in '07 (STL) posted a 1.00 GAA and a .968 save percentage with a win.

Goalie Stream of the Week

by Murray "Muzz" Kowalchuk

Yaroslav Askarov of SJ is the stream of the week, and Loosy Kuchy deserves the credit for it. One win, two goals against, a 2.01 GAA and a .920 save percentage on 23 saves from 25 shots. That's a clean line, which is all anyone asks of a stream, even if Loosy Kuchy dropped all three goalie categories anyway. Transaction Desk

by Murray "Muzz" Kowalchuk

The trade of the week is LL Cooley J getting Zach Werenski, Martin Pospisil and Nick Seeler from On The Bench Hockey 2.0, who took back Mika Zibanejad, Sam Reinhart and Brandt Clarke. Werenski is an elite offensive defenceman and the cornerstone of the deal, and Dennis picked up a depth piece in Seeler too. Jordan's side gets two proven scorers in Zibanejad and Reinhart plus a young defenceman in Clarke, so the case for his end has some legs. I still side with the team that got the best player. In a fourteen-team league, a top defenceman who fills every category is hard to replace.

The other two deals involve some big names. Loosy Kuchy sent Leon Draisaitl to Your team's Aho and received Nikita Kucherov. Both are superstars, and the winner will be whichever one stays healthier. Ducks on the Pond 🦆 got Mikhail Sergachev from Jetting out of Winnipeg, which received Juraj Slafkovský and Alexander Nikishin. Nikishin is listed as out as of the week's end, so he isn't providing anything right now. Sergachev is the more established name and I give Tom the edge.

On the FAAB side, Gavin Mackinaw was the only team to bid, laying out $56 on Zach Hyman of EDM. That's more than half of a $100 budget on one winger, and it leaves $44. Hyman is a proven scorer, but that's a big spend for a single player. Fortune Favors The Boldy has $0 remaining, Loosy Kuchy has $3 and Ducks on the Pond 🦆 has $7, so those three managers have almost no room for a goalie emergency. Eight teams haven't touched their $100 yet, and two more have most of theirs. Your team's Aho sits at $85 and LL Cooley J at $55.

The Fancy Stats Desk

by Dr. Ingrid Lindqvist

Ducks on the Pond 🦆 led the league in points with 49 and won the week 5-4-0. They did it with the league's worst goaltending: an 8.68 GAA and a .737 save percentage, against league averages of 3.19 and .882. The skaters carried them, with 15 goals and 34 assists, and Angels and Dmen took the goalie categories, plus hits 47 to 46, to win four categories and lose by one. Skater production this high rarely coincides with goaltending this bad, so one of the two lines will move toward the middle. A week like this is a small-sample distortion, and Tom should hope the goaltending corrects first.

On The Bench Hockey 2.0 was the volume team: 109 shots, 37 assists and 47 blocks, each the league high, plus 61 hits against an average of 49.64. They won 5-3-1 against Hacklin Celeweenie, but the margin was thinner than the shot totals suggest. Their goalies went 0 wins with a 4.36 GAA and a .844 save percentage. The teams had 11 goals each, and Hacklin Celeweenie had 93 shots to 109 and a 1.99 GAA and .925 save percentage with two wins. Their 28 hits were the league low, and that is what the loss came down to.

The goalie lines tell the cleaner story. Pasta La Vista went 8-0-1 behind a 1.01 GAA and a .971 save percentage, both league bests, and Loosy Kuchy could not find a way through. Against a league average of .882, that is the kind of number that regresses in a hurry. 50 in '07 posted a 1.50 GAA and a .950 save percentage and won 6-2-1, which tells me the best goaltending in the league is not a one-team phenomenon this week. Fortune Favors The Boldy had three goalie wins and a 1.33 GAA, enough to cover a 21-block week.

Then there is LL Cooley J, whose 23 goals against a league average of 12.21 was the highest figure in the league. They won 6-3-0 over Jetting out of Winnipeg, whose 65 shots were the league low. Winnipeg won the goalie categories, so the matchup came down to who held the volume. I admit that 23 goals on 107 shots was fun to watch. I will now correct that: it is a number that needs a bigger sample before anyone builds a plan on it.
